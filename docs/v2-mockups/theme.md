# Ryan and the Siege of Fernhollow

Draft for David to edit. Nothing in this file is locked.

This is the look-and-theme guide for the public game. Fernhollow lives in this public repo and deploys on GitHub Pages from main. It is not a private or family-only project. The original Geometry Arena repo stays unchanged. No new chiptune has been added yet; this build still plays the existing neon soundtrack.

## Title

**Ryan and the Siege of Fernhollow**

Alternates:

- **Knight Ryan of Fernhollow**
- **The Crown of Fernhollow**

## Kingdom

**Fernhollow** — a small green kingdom of ferns, cobblestone courtyards, and friendly stone castles. The game takes place in the castle courtyard, which stands in for the arena in the current game.

## Title-screen story

While the knights of Fernhollow were away in the far meadows, a band of mischievous forest creatures slipped into the castle courtyard and made off with the kingdom's golden crown. The courtyard is the heart of Fernhollow, and now wave after wave of odd little monsters come tumbling over the walls to keep the crown hidden. Only young Ryan, in his lucky green hoodie and a borrowed red neckerchief, is brave enough to stand in the middle of the stones and send them packing with sparks of sunlight-magic. He does not have to go looking for them — they come to him, faster and thicker as the night goes on. If Ryan can hold the courtyard, the crown will shine on Fernhollow once more.

## How the game still plays

Same twin-stick arena shooter as Geometry Arena, with a new coat of paint:

- Ryan stands in the courtyard, moves, aims, and fires magic bolts.
- Enemies come in waves. Density ramps over a run, with bursts and lulls.
- Scoop **Sunseeds** to raise a multiplier. At higher multipliers the bolts become twin, then triple (same idea as today's ×45 / ×110 gun upgrades).
- Start with 3 hearts and 3 Royal Bursts, caps at 5 each, same scarce extra-life cadence unless we change it later.
- Kid-friendly throughout: fun and brave, not scary or gory.

## Enemies

Names and looks below match the gameplay mockup where they appear.

1. **Pufflings** — Smiling round lime-green orbs with two little leaf-ears. They waddle in slowly, in packs. The first enemy you learn to clear.
2. **Thistle Jays** — Skinny purple birds with a bright thistle-crest. They dash in a straight line across the courtyard and don't turn until they hit a wall.
3. **Acorn Spinners** — Brown acorns with propeller caps. They spiral and orbit around Ryan instead of walking straight in.
4. **Split Sprouts** — Plump green sprout-orbs, cousins of the Pufflings. When hit, they pop into two smaller smiling sprouts. (The mockup shows the family resemblance; it does not clearly show a mid-split.)
5. **Moss Turtles** — Chunky, friendly turtles with mossy shells. Slow, take three hits, and soak up bolts at the front of a pack.
6. **Gust Blooms** — A smiling sunflower that plants itself and puffs a playful swirl of wind, gently pulling Ryan closer, then dropping gold Sunseeds.

## Boss idea

**Baron Bramble** — A big, slightly clumsy shrub-ogre with the stolen gold crown tilted sideways on his leafy head. He stomps (a ring of leaves), tosses acorn bombs, and calls in Pufflings. More blustery than scary. When he is beaten, the crown pops off and sparkles, and the courtyard bells ring.

## Power-ups

- **Sunseeds** — Tiny gold seeds left when enemies poof. Scoop them to raise the multiplier.
- **Royal Burst** — The bomb. A bright gold shockwave that clears the courtyard. No points, same as today's bomb. Start with 3, max 5.
- **Heart Apples** — Extra lives. Start with 3, max 5, still a treat when one appears.
- A hit still taxes the multiplier (keeps a recovery seed), so a mistake is a setback, not a total wipe.

## Music and sound (direction only)

Chiptune in a classic NES style: two square waves, a triangle bass, a noise channel for drums and poofs, and a simple lead.

- **Title:** about 100–108 BPM, warm major key. A short castle fanfare that settles into a storybook melody — a lullaby with a little march in it. Loopable 16 or 32 bars.
- **Gameplay:** about 136–144 BPM, bouncy and brave. An 8-bar hook that adds layers as the waves heat up (extra square arpeggio, busier noise snare). Stays playful, never horror-tense. Two variants that can crossfade every minute or so, like the current soundtrack.
- **Boss:** about 126–132 BPM. Heavier drums and a deeper triangle bass. A minor verse that lifts to major for the chorus, so it feels like a showdown Ryan can win, not a scare.

Sound effects:

- Magic bolt: short rising square blip
- Bolt hit: soft noise tick plus a tiny coin sparkle
- Enemy poof: a descending bubble / toy-box pop, kept quiet
- Sunseed scoop: a bright little coin
- Heart Apple: a short 3-note jingle
- Royal Burst: a deep noise whoosh, then a sparkle shower
- Player bump / lost heart: a gentle boing, not a harsh sting
- Wave clear: a one-bar fanfare
- Game over: a soft descending minor phrase, then the title motif
- Baron Bramble stomp: a low triangle thud plus a leaf-shake
- PRESS START confirm: a tiny accept blip
